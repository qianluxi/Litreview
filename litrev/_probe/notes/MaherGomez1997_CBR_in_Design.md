# MaherGomez1997_CBR_in_Design

- 条目 1：把 CBR 用于设计有两个主要考虑——设计案例的表示，以及用于召回和改编设计案例的过程模型。
  - "Two major considerations in a CBR approach to design are • representations of design cases and • process models for recalling and adapt- ing design cases." (Maher and Gomez, 1997, p.2)
- 条目 2：设计案例召回（design-case recall）被分解为索引、检索、选择三个子任务。
  - "it decomposes into the subtasks of indexing, retrieval, and selec- tion (see Figure 2a)." (Maher and Gomez, 1997, p.2)
- 条目 3：索引这一步做的是：确定以往的设计应具备哪些特征，才能与新的设计问题相关。
  - "Indexing identifies the features a previous design should have to be relevant to the new design problem." (Maher and Gomez, 1997, p.2)
