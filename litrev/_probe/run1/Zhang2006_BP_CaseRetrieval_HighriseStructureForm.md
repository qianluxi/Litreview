# Zhang2006_BP_CaseRetrieval_HighriseStructureForm

- 条目 1：论文针对高层结构智能选型中基于典型距离的传统案例检索方法不能自学习、智能化程度低的问题，提出并建立了基于改进 BP 神经网络的智能案例检索方法。
  - "First, analyzes the defects of the traditional case retrieval method (CRM) based on typical distance and the characteristics of architecture structure case retrieval, an improved BP-based intelligent CRM was established" (Zhang, 2006, p.1)
- 条目 2：案例用质量型、数量型与模糊型混合属性来刻画，检索匹配采用基于属性相对欧氏加权距离的混合相似度度量，以兼顾属性权重与量纲差异。
  - "To solve this problem and to be able to take into consideration everything important and necessary such as concurrence of quality, quantitative and fuzzy attributes and difference in dimension, this paper established a new similarity measure method based on the relative Euclidean weight distance of attributes (see expression (5))." (Zhang, 2006, p.2)
- 条目 3：论文承认网络拓扑结构的确定目前只有一些实验性结论，尚缺乏成熟、系统的理论，作者只能依据既有信息与检索需要自行设定网络结构。
  - "There are only some experimental conclusions concerning ascertainment of topological structure of networks instead of mature and systematic theory." (Zhang, 2006, p.2)
