# Napps2021_BIM_VariantRetrieval_CBR

- 条目 1：论文提出一种基于 IFC 标准、运用案例推理方法在建筑早期设计阶段检索合适变体的解决方案。
  - "This paper presents a solution for finding suitable variants in the early design phases of buildings, using the case-based reasoning (CBR) approach based on the IFC standard." (Napps, 2021, p.1)
- 条目 2：案例推理过程被表示为循环，Aamodt 与 Plaza 将其划分为检索、复用、修正与保留四个步骤。
  - "The process of case-based reasoning can be represented as a cycle, which Aamodt and Plaza [1] divide into four steps: retrieve, reuse, revise and retain." (Napps, 2021, p.2)
- 条目 3：论文提出面向建筑早期设计阶段检索过程的 BIM 解决方案。
  - "In this paper, a BIM-based solution for the retrieval process in the early design phase of a building is identified." (Napps, 2021, p.7)
