# Borner1993_StructuralSimilarity_CBR

- 抽取范围：全文 1–11 页

## 1. 一句话主张
- 论断：作者的核心主张是，只有在存在可据以把旧解正确改编到当前问题的知识时，才进行结构相似性评估。
- 引文："Structural similarity is assessed if and only if there is knowledge available to adapt old solutions correctly to the current problem of interest" (Borner, 1993, p.10)

## 2. 问题域
- 论断：方法本身不限于特定领域；示例取自工业建筑设计领域。
- 引文："Although our approach is more theoretical in nature and not restricted to a speci c domain we will give an example taken from the domain of industrial building design" (Borner, 1993, p.1)

## 3. 案例或数据的表示形式
- 论断：案例中的对象由空间尺寸和另外九个属性表示。
- 引文："Following the work of Ludger Hovestadt Hov each object circle or ellipse will be represented by its spatial dimensions and nine further attributes like time at which this object was created aspect which assumes one of return air fresh air etc and morphology which refers to access connection etc" (Borner, 1993, p.5)

## 4. 相似性或检索机制
- 论断：对新问题的初始分析可以通过基于属性描述的、成本不高的表面相似性评估来完成。
- 引文："The rst initial analysis of the new problem can be done by an inexpensive surface similarity assessment based on the attribute based descriptions" (Borner, 1993, p.6)

## 5. 系统形态
- 论断：SynTerm 与 SynGraph 为系统提供专项设计问题的解法。
- 引文："SynTerm and SynGraph supply the system with solutions for special design problems" (Borner, 1993, p.9)

## 6. 评价方式
- 论断：评价方式是报告初期实验结果对上述主张的支持。
- 引文："The initial experimental results support the claims that the proposed ap proach o ers improved adaptation facilities in synthesis tasks together with a greater overall explanation performance" (Borner, 1993, p.10)

## 7. 明确写出的局限
- 论断：作者明确写出，修改规则条件或权重的学习在本实现中未被处理。
- 引文："Learning e g the storage of new cases new rules and the application of techniques like Knuth Bendix completion to guarantee completeness of struc tural knowledge or learning of modi cation rule conditions or weights are not addressed in this implementation" (Borner, 1993, p.10)
