# LinRonghui2020_economicanalysisfloor

- 抽取范围：全文 p.1–6（正文见 p.2–6；p.1 为封面页，p.6 含参考文献）

## 1. 一句话主张
- 论断：对采用薄壁波纹管空心无梁楼盖的地下车库，其工程综合成本比采用主次梁楼盖结构者低 15% 以上。
- 引文："For the underground garage with the thin-wall corrugated pipe hollow beamless floor, the comprehensive cost of the project is more than 15% lower than that with the main and secondary beam floor structure." (LinRonghui, 2020, p.4)

## 2. 问题域
- 论断：本文评估地下车库顶板在薄壁波纹管空心无梁楼盖结构体系、普通梁板结构体系与无梁楼盖结构之间作选择时的经济效益。
- 引文："This paper studied economic efficiency of selecting hollow (thin-walled corrugated pipe) beamless floor structure system, common beam slab structure system and beamless floor structure for the roof of underground garage." (LinRonghui, 2020, p.2)

## 3. 案例或数据的表示形式
- 论断：两个楼盖方案的对比数据以按工程量与定额计算得到的经济指标（表 1）形式给出。
- 引文："The economic indexes of the thin-walled corrugated pipe hollow beamless floor and the traditional primary and secondary beam floor are obtained through the calculation of engineering quantity and quota (Table 1)." (LinRonghui, 2020, p.3)

## 4. 相似性或检索机制
- 论断：未涉及

## 5. 系统形态
- 论断：本文结合实际工程案例，把薄壁波纹管空心无梁楼盖与其他常见楼盖结构方案作对比，得出薄壁波纹管空心无梁楼盖的经济性能。
- 引文："Combined with the actual engineering case, the paper compares the thin wall corrugated pipe hollow beamless floor with other common floor structure schemes, and obtains the economic performance of the thin wall corrugated pipe hollow beamless floor." (LinRonghui, 2020, p.3)

## 6. 评价方式
- 论断：评价通过薄壁波纹管空心无梁楼盖与主次梁楼盖之间的对比和预算造价分析进行，得出该技术在综合经济效益上优势明显。
- 引文："Through the comparison and budget cost analysis between the thin-wall corrugated pipe hollow beamless floor and the main and secondary beam floor, the advantages of the thin-wall corrugated pipe hollow beamless floor technology in the comprehensive economic benefits are obvious." (LinRonghui, 2020, p.4)

## 7. 明确写出的局限
- 论断：作者指出，从楼面造价看，薄壁波纹管空心无梁楼盖相比平楼盖并无优势，造价略高于平楼盖。
- 引文："From the perspective of floor cost, the thin-walled corrugated hollow beamless floor has no advantage over the flat slab, and the cost is slightly higher than the flat slab." (LinRonghui, 2020, p.6)
