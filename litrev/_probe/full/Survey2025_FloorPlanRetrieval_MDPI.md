# Survey2025_FloorPlanRetrieval_MDPI

- 抽取范围：全文 1–27 页

## 1. 一句话主张
- 论断：该文全面梳理建筑平面图检索所依赖的语义、空间、形状、纹理四类特征（合称 3ST 特征）。
- 引文："This paper presents a comprehensive exploration of four primary features essential for the retrieval of building floor plans: semantic features, spatial features, shape features, and texture features (collectively referred to as 3ST features)." (Ling et al., 2025, p.1)

## 2. 问题域
- 论断：问题域是建筑平面图的特征检索，并置于建筑工程项目管理与执行的语境中。
- 引文："Feature retrieval technology for building floor plans has garnered significant attention in recent years due to its critical role in the efficient management and execution of construction projects." (Ling et al., 2025, p.1)

## 3. 案例或数据的表示形式
- 论断：平面图以高层语义特征与特征向量的形式表示，并用特征向量计算图像相似度。
- 引文："By identifying unique high-level semantic features and employing feature vectors to calculate image similarity, this approach enables effective retrieval of building floor plans." (Ling et al., 2025, p.3)

## 4. 相似性或检索机制
- 论断：检索以树编辑距离作为衡量查询相似度的度量。
- 引文："Floor plan retrieval is then executed using tree editing distance as a metric for query similarity." (Ling et al., 2025, p.14)

## 5. 系统形态
- 论断：该文是综述，考察这些特征检索方法并提出面向未来研究的融合技术。
- 引文："This paper thoroughly examines these feature retrieval methods and introduces advanced fusion techniques to support future research" (Ling et al., 2025, p.23)

## 6. 评价方式
- 论断：常用评价指标包括 accuracy、F1 score、mAP 与 Matthews 相关系数 MCC。
- 引文："Commonly used evaluation metrics include accuracy, F1 score, mean average precision (mAP), and the Matthews correlation coefficient (MCC) [75]." (Ling et al., 2025, p.20)

## 7. 明确写出的局限
- 论断：作者指出样本数量有限的数据集可能不足以代表所有建筑类型与设计风格，从而限制研究结论的普适性。
- 引文："Datasets with a limited number of samples may not adequately represent all possible building types and design styles, thereby constraining the generalizability of the research findings." (Ling et al., 2025, p.20)
